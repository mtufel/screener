Backtest Report — Strategy 2

Extreme LTF FVG
Backtest Findings

BTC 30-day historical simulation (15m LTF, wick invalidation). 23 trades — negative expectancy, large drawdown, and a clear configuration fix.

Symbol BTC · LTF 15m · 30 days · 2026-09-04 · backtest_extreme_fvg.py

The baseline is broken

The default Strategy 2 configuration loses on every target tier: 43.5% win at 1R, 17.4% at 2R, 13.0% at 3R. Net realized PnL is -3.0R (1R), expanding to -11.0R at 2R/3R. The maximum drawdown of -14.0R against only 23 trades is severe — roughly 61% of total risk capital drawn down in a single sequence.

Critical findingPrice reaches the target zone (+1.46R avg MFE) and then reverses into the stop. The strategy has positive excursion but negative realization — entry selection is picking weak FVGs that lack follow-through.

What the variants reveal

Six configurations were tested. Only two produced positive expectancy:

1h LTF + close invalidation yielded +2.0R net, 60% win rate, PF 1.50, and only -3.0R drawdown — the best combined risk/return profile. ETH at 15m/wick produced +5.0R with 59.3% win rate, confirming the strategy works better on different symbols than BTC at this granularity.

The worst outcome came from min-gap 0.1% (PF 0.40, -13R drawdown) — tightening the filter removed too many setups and left only the weakest survivors.

Backtest bug — double counting

Per docs/strategy2-findings.md §2.1, the backtest engine counts the same trade as both a win and a loss when a trade touches 2R/3R and later stops out. The sticky hit_1r/hit_2r/hit_3r flags mean wins_2r and losses can both increment for a single execution. Treat all stats as approximate until fixed.

Recommendations (priority order)

  1. Pause live BTC 15m/wick deployments. Negative expectancy with -14R drawdown — not a risk-management problem, a setup-selection problem.
  2. Adopt 1h LTF + close invalidation for new setups. Best verified profile across all tested configurations.
  3. Test ETH or other symbols at 15m before abandoning that granularity. ETH already shows positive results in default mode.
  4. Do not raise min-gap above 0.05%. Higher filtering removed too many valid setups; only weak FVGs survived.
  5. Fix backtest double-count (docs §2.1) before relying on any percentage — corrupts win-rate, PF, and net-R across the board.
  6. Address stale PENDING ledger entries (docs §2.2). They suppress new alerts and inflate tracked-trade counts.
