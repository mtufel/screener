What the Data Says

1h produces the best risk-adjusted returns. It has the lowest max drawdown (−3R vs −6R / −9R), the highest 2R profit factor (2.40), the best 2R win rate (54.5%), and the highest MFE per trade (+2.32R). It trades less often (11 setups vs 73) but each setup has high conviction — average hold ~20 hours, aligning with 4H-anchor-to-LTF-FVG momentum.

5m delivers absolute volume. 73 trades in 30 days, best absolute 2R (+29R) and 3R (+27R), but drawdown is double (−6R) and win rate lower (47% at 2R). Good for high-frequency strategies with tighter risk limits.

15m underperforms. Negative net PnL at 1R/2R, worst drawdown (−9R), lowest win rate (27% at 2R). Not recommended for production with close invalidation.

1m was not completed (too slow; ~2–3 min/run, 30-day runs exceed practical limits for this session). It would likely generate the most trades but with higher noise.

The session/weekday filters are working correctly. They are backtest-only for now and config-driven via EXTREME_SESSION_FILTER_ENABLED / EXTREME_WEEKDAY_FILTER_ENABLED env vars. They eliminate low-liquidity overnight/weekend setups, improving quality but reducing count.

Production Recommendation

PRODUCTION Use 1h + CLOSE invalidation. Your .env already has this configured (EXTREME_LTF_TIMEFRAME=1h, EXTREME_USE_CLOSE_INVALIDATION=true). The 1h config delivers the cleanest capital preservation (−3R DD) with best per-trade quality (+2.32R MFE, 2.40 PF at 2R).

A/B TEST Session/weekday filters on 30d. Run python3 backtest_extreme_fvg.py --symbol BTC --days 30 --ltf 1h --invalidation close --session-filter --weekday-filter to see filtered vs unfiltered comparison over a full month.

Do not deploy filters to live strategy engine yet. They are backtest-only per the spec (OpenSpec change extreme-session-weekday-filters).
