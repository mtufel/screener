2-Stage Strategy Execution Rules

1. 4H FVG Cache & Invalidation

Maintains active 4H FVGs on finished candles. Automatically purges FVGs if price breaks past (below bottom for Bullish, above top for Bearish).

2. Stage 1: Setup Formed

When price enters 4H FVG, watches for a new 1m/5m/15m FVG in the same direction. Alert 1 is dispatched to prepare for entry.

3. Stage 2: Trade Activation

Once price retraces back into the new LTF FVG, the trade triggers with SL at candle extremes and 1R, 1.5R, 2R, 3R TP targets!
